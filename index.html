How to order

No sign-up and no payment on the site. Your order reaches us as a WhatsApp message.

  1. 1

    Choose dishes

    Tap “Add” — you can change the quantity right on the card.

  2. 2

    Check your cart

    Enter your name and phone, and your address if you need delivery.

  3. 3

    Send it on WhatsApp

    We put together the message with your dishes and total — you just tap “Send”.

  4. 4

    Get your order

    We reply, confirm the time and deliver. Or pick it up yourself.
